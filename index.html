About Us

A perfect blend of creativity, clarity and communication.

The Exceptional Art & Prints is a Lagos based creative studio helping businesses bring their branding and physical marketing materials to life, from packaging and shopping bags to labels, ribbons and print collateral.

We work closely with every client to understand what their brand needs to say, then design and produce materials that communicate it clearly, consistently and beautifully, so your customers notice, remember and come back.

Every project moves through the same care: a clear conversation, thoughtful design, and production that respects your brand down to the finish.

FAQ

Good to know.

What services do you offer?+

We create custom packaging and boxes, branded shopping bags, business branding, labels and product packaging, promotional printing, branded ribbons, marketing and print materials, and custom design and production work.

Can I provide my own design?+

Yes. If you already have a design ready, share it with us and we'll produce it to your specification.

Can you help with my design?+

Yes. If you don't have a design yet, tell us your idea and our team will design it for you as part of our custom design and production service.

How do I place an order or request an enquiry?+

Fill in the enquiry form on this website, message us on WhatsApp, or send us an email. Tell us what you need and we'll take it from there.

Do you deliver?+

Delivery details are arranged directly with our team based on your order and location. Reach out via WhatsApp or the enquiry form and we'll confirm what works for you.

How can I contact you?+

You can reach us by WhatsApp on 0809 374 6831, by email at hello@theexceptionalart.com, or through the enquiry form below. We're based in Lagos, Nigeria.
